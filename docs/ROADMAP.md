# Roadmap

## Shipped
- 0.1: autonomous residents, construction, policies, local saves.
- 0.2: procedural coastal 3D city, camera, lighting, 2D fallback.
- 0.3: inventory, business capital, output, social contacts, decision inspector, portable saves, modular source.
- 0.4: weighted A* grid routing, trip duration, transit policy, aggregate road demand, household groups and rent, age progression, four seeded scenarios, 13×13 and 19×19 maps, first-person walking, weather, local resident conversations, resident search, advisor and objectives.

## Remaining
- Dedicated vehicle and pedestrian road networks, intersection logic and transit routes.
- Full family relationships and life cycles, business formation, detailed housing and labor market.
- Streaming terrain, richer art assets, scenario editor, replay, device profiling and accessibility testing.
- Optional secure LLM backend, shared world and cloud persistence.
- Commercial-quality QA, packaging, distribution and monetization.

The simulation is a local sandbox, not a validated economic model. Static hosting does not provide shared multiplayer or LLM inference. Browser/WebGL acceptance testing is required before claiming production-grade visual quality.
