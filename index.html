RESIDENT CONVERSATION · LOCAL RULES

Welcome to Atlas

You are the mayor of a living city. Residents make independent decisions based on hunger, energy, money, happiness and health. Your decisions change their world.

Build homes and jobs, set taxes and services, inspect citizens, and run experiments. Use the speed controls to advance time, or pause to plan. Choose a scenario, manage transit and rent, and complete mayor objectives. Walk mode lets you explore the streets with WASD or the arrow keys. Resident conversations are locally generated from their actual simulation state, not an LLM.

No sign-in or API key required. Save stores your city on this device only.
